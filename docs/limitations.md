# Limitations

- No wet-lab validation is included.
- Antibody immobilization is simplified as an idealized finite surface site density.
- Debye screening in physiological salt may reduce effective surface-charge coupling.
- GFET behavior is approximated for COMSOL compatibility and may not capture all graphene-specific transport physics.
- Implantability, packaging, immune response, and biocompatibility are future work.
- Final sensitivity and LOD claims depend on the selected noise floor and coupling efficiency.
