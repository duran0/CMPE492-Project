# Five-Minute Video Script

## 0:00-0:30 Problem

Introduce HER2 as a breast cancer biomarker and explain why continuous or rapid detection is valuable.

## 0:30-1:15 Proposed Sensor

Explain the antibody-functionalized GFET concept and how bound HER2 can affect drain-source current.

## 1:15-2:30 COMSOL Model

Describe transport, surface binding, and electrical response stages. Emphasize that the model separates transport limits from transistor response.

## 2:30-3:45 Results

Show concentration profiles, bound HER2 trends, `Delta I_DS` response curves, and sensitivity/LOD outputs.

## 3:45-4:30 Limitations

Mention no wet-lab validation, simplified antibody immobilization, Debye screening, and GFET modeling approximations.

## 4:30-5:00 Conclusion

Conclude with the main engineering insight and final deliverables.
